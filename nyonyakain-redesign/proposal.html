01 · Overview

1Our understanding

Nyonya Kain needs a cleanup and organisation of its existing website, not a full rebuild. The goal is a site that is tidy, easy to navigate, works properly on desktop and mobile, and is handed over with clear documentation so the owner can update products, pages and articles independently.

Website

nyonyakain.com

Existing live site

Platform

WordPress + WooCommerce

Existing theme retained

Hosting

Hostinger

Current provider, no migration

2Approach

Safety first

  • Full backup of files and database before any change
  • Changes tested on a staging copy where practical
  • Approval requested before deleting content or touching checkout

Keep what works

  • Existing theme, orders, customers and URLs preserved
  • Any change that may affect SEO flagged in advance
  • Product data kept intact unless change approved

Owner in control

  • Ownership of domain, hosting and licences confirmed
  • All access and credentials documented
  • Owner trained to run routine updates

Clear communication

  • Audit findings shared before work begins
  • Change log kept throughout the project
  • Out-of-scope items priced separately, in writing

02 · Scope of work

3Proposed tasks

A

Initial Technical Audit & Access Review

  • Review WordPress, WooCommerce, hosting, domain, DNS, SSL and plugins
  • Identify access, ownership, renewal or technical issues (Hostinger & domain)
  • List themes, plugins and paid licences in use
  • Written summary of current status, limitations, extra costs and recommended next steps, before work begins
B

Website Backup & Safe Working Process

  • Full backup (files + database) before any change; downloadable copy for the owner
  • Staging/test environment via Hostinger where available
  • Preserve content, orders, customer info, URLs and configuration
  • Written approval before deleting content or changing live orders/checkout
C

Product & Image Organisation

  • Clean up up to 20 existing WooCommerce product listings
  • Organise categories, titles, images and galleries
  • Remove/correct duplicate, incorrect or poorly sized images (replacements supplied by owner)
  • Check descriptions, prices, variations and key information; compress images for faster loading

02 · Scope of work
E

Functionality & Usability Checks

  • Desktop and mobile testing of navigation, menus, links and images
  • Contact links, WhatsApp buttons and enquiry forms verified
  • Product pages, cart and checkout tested (safe test method if store is active)
  • Corporate gifting and reseller enquiry pathways tested end to end
  • Broken links, display issues and technical errors fixed or reported
  • Existing URLs preserved; any SEO-affecting change flagged
F

Training, Documentation & Handover

  • 60–90 minute live screen-shared training
  • Recording of the session for future reference
  • Simple written guide: add/edit products, replace images, update pages, publish articles
  • Guidance on backups, routine updates and when to get professional help
  • Summary of hosting, domain, plugin & licence arrangements incl. renewals and costs
  • Change log, outstanding issues and recommended next steps
  • Confirmation owner controls website, domain, hosting, content, backups and accounts
  • 14-day post-handover period to fix defects from agreed work

Exclusions

Complete redesign, custom theme development, custom reseller login portal, inventory or CRM integrations, extensive copywriting, professional photography, major data recovery, complex migration, paid plugins/software subscriptions, and complicated ownership recovery. Any work outside the agreed scope will be identified, priced and approved in writing before it begins.
